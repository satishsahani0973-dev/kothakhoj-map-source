This page explains what KothaKhoj collects, when and why, and how it is kept. We keep it short and honest.

1. What we collect, and when

  • When you sign in — your username and password. Accounts for adding rooms (old students, colleges and landlords) are created by KothaKhoj. Your username is shown publicly next to every room you add ("added by ..."), so choose one you are happy to display. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • When you add a room — its place on the map, type, rent, photos, when it will be free, details, and an optional phone number. These are shown publicly, by your choice. Do not enter a phone number you are not willing to show.
  • Your promise — when you add a room you confirm that you are 18 or older and that the owner agreed to the listing. We keep that confirmation, with the date of the wording you agreed to, privately with the room. It is never shown publicly.
  • When you comment — the comment and the name you sign it with are public.

2. Why we collect it

Only to help students find rooms and to let people who have a room reach them. We do not use it for anything else.

3. Collected automatically

  • Session cookie — keeps you signed in, and lets the site remember the rooms you added during your visit.
  • Browser storage — your "Continue browsing" choice and the list of rooms you created stay in your own browser. They never leave your device.
  • Location — if you use the locate or directions feature, your device's location is used in your browser to centre the map and work out routes. Your live location is never stored on our servers.
  • Error reports — if something breaks on our server, the technical details of that request are sent to Sentry, an error-tracking service, so we can fix it.

4. How it is checked

We do not visit or verify every room. The person adding it confirms it is real, that the owner agreed, and that they are 18 or older. Anyone can report a room with the "not real?" link, and we review every report.

5. How it is protected, and where it is kept

  • The site only works over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and our server can only be reached through Cloudflare's security network.
  • Only the person who runs KothaKhoj can access the stored data.
  • The site runs on a DigitalOcean server in Bengaluru, India. Photos and backups are stored in Cloudflare R2. Your information is therefore stored outside Nepal.
  • Backups — a private copy of the database is made every hour when something has changed, and kept for at least 90 days. One copy a month is kept longer, for recovery. Backups are never public.

6. Services we use

Mapbox (the map, search and directions — coordinates are sent to Mapbox to calculate results), Cloudflare (security, speed, and storing photos and backups), DigitalOcean (our server), Sentry (error reports), and a published Google Sheet that provides the list of colleges on the map.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not send you marketing messages.
  • We do not track you across other websites.

8. Removing your information

You can delete a room you added with the delete button on it — the room and its photos are deleted together. A copy can still load for up to a day from a link someone already had, and remains in our private backups until they expire. If you cannot reach the room, or want any information about you removed, email nepalkothakhoj@gmail.com and we will remove it within 48 hours.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
